Medicinal Herbs / Adaptogens

Tulsi (Holy Basil)

Botanical Name: Ocimum tenuiflorum

Family: Lamiaceae

Natural Habitat: Cultivated across Indian subcontinent; thrives in warm, subtropical climates with moist soil.

View in 3D Garden Take Plant Quiz

🍃 Ayurvedic Taxonomy & Energetics

Rasa (Taste): Tikta (Bitter), Katu (Pungent) | Virya (Potency): Ushna (Hot) | Vipaka: Katu

Key Active Phytochemicals

Eugenol, Ursolic acid, Rosmarinic acid, Carvacrol, Linalool, β-caryophyllene.

⚕️ Therapeutic Applications & Uses

  • Potent immunomodulatory agent enhancing natural killer (NK) cell activity.
  • Relieves respiratory congestion, asthma, bronchitis, and chronic cough.
  • Adaptogenic properties reduce psychological stress and normalize cortisol levels.
  • Antimicrobial efficacy against oral pathogens, supporting dental health.

⚠️ Precautions & Safety Advisory

Avoid large doses during early pregnancy; monitor if taking anticoagulant medication.
