Digestive & Circulatory Stimulant

Ginger (Adrak / Shunthi)

Botanical Name: Zingiber officinale

Family: Zingiberaceae

Natural Habitat: Humid tropical and subtropical rainforest climates.

View in 3D Garden Take Plant Quiz

🍃 Ayurvedic Taxonomy & Energetics

Rasa: Katu | Virya: Ushna | Vipaka: Madhura | Ignites Agni (digestive fire), destroys Ama

Key Active Phytochemicals

Gingerols, Shogaols, Zingiberene, Paradols, Bisabolene.

⚕️ Therapeutic Applications & Uses

  • Effective anti-emetic relieving motion sickness, morning sickness, and chemotherapy nausea.
  • Stimulates gastric motility, gastric emptying, and salivary enzymes.
  • Relieves dysmenorrhea and muscular soreness with systemic analgesic action.
  • Peripheral vasodilation improving microcirculation and cardiac efficiency.

⚠️ Precautions & Safety Advisory

Use caution in patients with active gallstones or taking high-dose blood thinners.
