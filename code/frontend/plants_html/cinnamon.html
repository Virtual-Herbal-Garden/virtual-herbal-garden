Metabolic & Cardiovascular Tonic

Cinnamon (Dalchini)

Botanical Name: Cinnamomum verum

Family: Lauraceae

Natural Habitat: Sri Lanka, coastal Southern India, and tropical Asia.

View in 3D Garden Take Plant Quiz

🍃 Ayurvedic Taxonomy & Energetics

Rasa: Madhura, Tikta, Katu | Virya: Ushna | Vipaka: Madhura | Balances Kapha and Vata

Key Active Phytochemicals

Cinnamaldehyde, Cinnamic acid, Proanthocyanidins, Eugenol.

⚕️ Therapeutic Applications & Uses

  • Mimics insulin function and activates cellular glucose uptake via GLUT-4 transporters.
  • Lowers total cholesterol, LDL, and triglyceride levels in metabolic dysregulation.
  • Strong anti-biofilm properties against resistant bacterial and fungal colonies.
  • Enhances cerebral microcirculation and protects dopaminergic neurons.

⚠️ Precautions & Safety Advisory

Prefer Ceylon cinnamon over Cassia to avoid high coumarin accumulation.
