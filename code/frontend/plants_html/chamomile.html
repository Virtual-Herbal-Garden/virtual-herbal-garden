Nervine Sedative & Spasmolytic

Chamomile (Babuna)

Botanical Name: Matricaria chamomilla

Family: Asteraceae

Natural Habitat: Sunlit temperate fields and meadows across Europe, Western Asia, and Northern India.

View in 3D Garden Take Plant Quiz

🍃 Ayurvedic Taxonomy & Energetics

Rasa: Tikta | Virya: Sheeta | Pacifies Pitta and high nervous excitability

Key Active Phytochemicals

Apigenin, Chamazulene, α-bisabolol, Flavonoids (Quercetin, Luteolin).

⚕️ Therapeutic Applications & Uses

  • Apigenin binds to benzodiazepine GABA-A receptors, inducing tranquil restful sleep.
  • Relieves gastrointestinal spasms, colic, flatulence, and nervous stomach.
  • Topical anti-inflammatory soothing eczema, dermatitis, and conjunctivitis.
  • Reduces histamine release and allergic mucosal inflammation.

⚠️ Precautions & Safety Advisory

Avoid if allergic to plants of the Asteraceae (daisy/ragweed) family.
