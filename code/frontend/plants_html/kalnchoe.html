Lithotriptic & Nephroprotective

Pashanbheda / Miracle Leaf

Botanical Name: Kalanchoe pinnata

Family: Crassulaceae

Natural Habitat: Warm tropical regions; famously propagates plantlets along leaf margins.

View in 3D Garden Take Plant Quiz

🍃 Ayurvedic Taxonomy & Energetics

Rasa: Kashaya, Tikta | Virya: Sheeta | Ashmari Bhedana (dissolves urinary calculi)

Key Active Phytochemicals

Bufadienolides (Bryophyllin A, B, C), Flavonoids (Quercitrin), Phenolic acids.

⚕️ Therapeutic Applications & Uses

  • Proven lithotriptic activity that inhibits calcium oxalate crystal nucleation in kidneys.
  • Reduces urinary tract inflammation, burning micturition, and edema.
  • Accelerates chronic skin ulcer healing and wound contracture.
  • Immunomodulatory and hepatoprotective properties against toxic chemicals.

⚠️ Precautions & Safety Advisory

Cardiac glycoside derivatives require moderate, supervised therapeutic dosages.
