Rasayana / Rejuvenative & Nootropic

Ashwagandha (Indian Ginseng)

Botanical Name: Withania somnifera

Family: Solanaceae

Natural Habitat: Dry regions of India, Middle East, and North Africa.

View in 3D Garden Take Plant Quiz

🍃 Ayurvedic Taxonomy & Energetics

Rasa: Tikta, Kashaya (Astringent), Madhura | Virya: Ushna | Balances Vata and Kapha

Key Active Phytochemicals

Withanolides (Withaferin A, Withanolide D), Alkaloids (Withanine, Somniferine), Sitoindosides.

⚕️ Therapeutic Applications & Uses

  • Regulates HPA axis activity, lowering chronic serum cortisol and anxiety.
  • Improves cardiorespiratory endurance (VO2 max) and physical muscle recovery.
  • Enhances cognitive function, memory consolidation, and executive attention.
  • Promotes restorative slow-wave sleep via GABAergic pathways.

⚠️ Precautions & Safety Advisory

Caution in autoimmune conditions (e.g. Hashimoto's) and concurrent thyroid medication.
