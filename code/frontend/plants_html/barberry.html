Hepatobiliary & Glycemic Regulator

Barberry / Daruharidra

Botanical Name: Berberis vulgaris

Family: Berberidaceae

Natural Habitat: Himalayan slopes, temperate shrubs across Northern India and Eurasia.

View in 3D Garden Take Plant Quiz

🍃 Ayurvedic Taxonomy & Energetics

Rasa: Tikta, Kashaya | Virya: Ushna | Chakshushya (eye health), Yakrit Uttejaka

Key Active Phytochemicals

Berberine, Berbamine, Oxyacanthine, Jatrorrhizine, Palmatine.

⚕️ Therapeutic Applications & Uses

  • Berberine activates AMPK pathway, regulating cellular energy metabolism and lipid homeostasis.
  • Enhances insulin receptor expression, rivaling pharmaceutical insulin sensitizers.
  • Cleanses bile ducts, protects hepatocytes, and inhibits fatty liver progression.
  • Ophthalmic astringent used in traditional formulations for inflammatory eye conditions.

⚠️ Precautions & Safety Advisory

Not recommended during pregnancy or lactation due to uterine stimulation.
