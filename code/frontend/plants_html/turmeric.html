Anti-inflammatory & Hepatoprotective

Turmeric (Haridra)

Botanical Name: Curcuma longa

Family: Zingiberaceae

Natural Habitat: Native to Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia; requires rich, loamy, well-drained soil.

View in 3D Garden Take Plant Quiz

🍃 Ayurvedic Taxonomy & Energetics

Rasa: Tikta, Katu | Virya: Ushna | Vipaka: Katu | Purifies blood, balances all three doshas

Key Active Phytochemicals

Curcuminoids (Curcumin, Demethoxycurcumin, Bisdemethoxycurcumin), Turmerone, Zingiberene.

⚕️ Therapeutic Applications & Uses

  • Inhibits NF-κB inflammatory signaling and COX-2 enzyme expression.
  • Potent joint mobility enhancement in osteoarthritis and rheumatoid inflammation.
  • Hepatoprotective and bile secretion stimulation for liver detoxification.
  • Topical wound antiseptic promoting re-epithelialization.

⚠️ Precautions & Safety Advisory

Enhance absorption by pairing with piperine (black pepper) or healthy fats.
