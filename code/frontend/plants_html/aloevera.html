Dermatological & Digestive Herbs

Aloe Vera (Ghritkumari)

Botanical Name: Aloe barbadensis miller

Family: Asphodelaceae

Natural Habitat: Arid, tropical, and warm temperate regions worldwide.

View in 3D Garden Take Plant Quiz

🍃 Ayurvedic Taxonomy & Energetics

Rasa: Tikta, Madhura | Virya: Sheeta (Cooling) | Vipaka: Madhura | Tridoshic balancer

Key Active Phytochemicals

Aloin, Acemannan, Anthraquinones, Glucomannan, Vitamins A, C, E, and B12.

⚕️ Therapeutic Applications & Uses

  • Accelerates dermal wound healing, collagen synthesis, and first/second-degree burn recovery.
  • Soothes gastrointestinal mucosal inflammation, acid reflux, and IBS symptoms.
  • Deep topical hydration and UV protection with anti-inflammatory glycoproteins.
  • Supports healthy blood glucose regulation in metabolic syndrome.

⚠️ Precautions & Safety Advisory

Inner leaf latex should not be consumed in high amounts due to laxative anthraquinones.
